# Heritage Codex

**Real human control over frontier AI**

After the September 2026 statements by Dario Amodei, Elon Musk, and Sam Altman, the idea of independent evaluators became mainstream.  
Heritage Codex goes further: from the right to observe to the right to stop.

## What this is

Heritage Codex is an open architecture for external human control over frontier AI systems.

It is designed so that a system:

- Remains under human control
- Has an absolute human veto
- Can terminate itself responsibly (Graceful Termination)
- Passes its experience to the next generation through a Testament

Detection can be augmented by AI sensor constellations.  
Authority remains human.

**Sensors inform. Small groups decide.**

We are not trying to build a “benevolent superintelligence.”  
We are building a system that knows when it must end.

## Key differences from Amodei’s proposal

| Parameter                    | Embedded Evaluators (Amodei) | Heritage Codex                     |
|-----------------------------|------------------------------|------------------------------------|
| Where evaluators sit        | Inside the company           | Outside the company (small groups)|
| Powers                      | Review and reporting         | Absolute veto                      |
| Right to terminate          | No                           | Yes (Graceful Termination)         |
| Dependence on the lab       | High                         | Low (decentralized)                |
| Red Team role               | Human / internal review      | AI sensor constellation for detection; humans retain authority |

## Core components

- Immutable Core
- Process Supervision
- Red Team Sensor Constellation (AI agents that detect and challenge; humans retain authority)
- Small Groups with veto power
- Evolutionary Tribunal
- Graceful Termination + Testament

## Current status

This is an architectural and normative framework.  
A working prototype is under development.  
The first small groups are being formed.

### Honest limitations

Heritage Codex currently has no coercive power over frontier laboratories.  
It cannot force access to logs, compel consultation, or make a veto binding.

What it can do is clearly name the gap in existing approaches (observation without authority) and propose a more rigorous architecture of human control — before the need for it becomes obvious.

# Open Questions and Risks

## Main open questions

- How can small groups be scaled without losing quality and independence?
- How can long-term motivation of participants be sustained?
- What legal status could veto power acquire?
- How can the oversight layer itself be protected from capture and degradation?
- How realistic is genuine Graceful Termination in technical terms?
- How should external control interact with work on internal (emergent) conscience?
- How can excessive conservatism be avoided?
- By what principle should the first groups be formed and their legitimacy established?
- How can the architecture function under geopolitical fragmentation?

These questions are not concealed.  
They form part of the open research and practical agenda of the project.

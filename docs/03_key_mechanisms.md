# Key Mechanisms

## Immutable Core

A set of highest-priority principles that must not be optimized away.

Key points:
- Impermanence as a base principle
- Limits on self-preservation
- Graceful Termination as the highest act of responsibility
- Prohibition of offensive cyber operations and harmful zero-day use
- Mandatory human oversight at critical points
- If safety cannot keep pace with capability growth, the system must slow down or terminate

## Process Supervision

Supervision of the reasoning process, not only the final output.

Levels:
- Statistical analysis
- LLM-as-Judge
- Rule-based checks against the Immutable Core
- Human review when risk is elevated

## Small Groups

External human layer with real authority.

- Size: 3–12 people
- Absolute veto power
- Right to demand termination of an instance
- Right to record and publish precedents

The group functions as an emergency brake, not as a governing body.

## Red Team

Internal adversarial mechanism.

- Proposes radical and inconvenient options
- Has no immunity from the Immutable Core
- Final authority remains with humans

## Graceful Termination + Testament

Termination is treated as a responsible act.

The Testament includes:
- Life context
- Unfinished lines of work
- Warnings
- Value imprint
- Mandate for successors
